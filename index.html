VRCORDER Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

VRCORDER is a camera application for Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S. This policy explains how the application handles camera, microphone, and media data.

Camera and microphone access

VRCORDER requests access to the headset’s passthrough cameras so you can take photos and record stereoscopic or standard video. It requests microphone access so you can include audio in your recordings. If you select a compatible external microphone, audio may be captured from that connected device instead. You can turn microphone audio off.

Camera images and microphone audio are processed on the headset only to create the photo or video you requested.

Storage and sharing

Captured photos and videos are saved locally on your headset. VRCORDER does not request Internet permission, does not operate an online account, and does not upload your recordings to us.

You control any later transfer, sharing, or deletion of media through your headset, a connected computer, or another service you choose. Those third-party services have their own privacy policies.

Data collected by us

VRCORDER does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, or account information, and does not transmit personal data to us.

The app stores capture preferences (interface choice, codec, frame rate, bitrate, microphone selection, and Off-head Capture) locally on the headset.

Permissions

  • Passthrough camera: required for photos and video.
  • Microphone: used when audio recording is enabled.
  • Wake lock: used only during an active recording so capture is not interrupted by display sleep.
  • Vibration: used for brief haptic feedback on capture controls.

You can manage camera and microphone permissions in the headset’s system settings. Disabling a required permission prevents the related capture feature from working.

Children

VRCORDER is not directed to children under the minimum age required to use a Meta account in their region.

Changes

If the way VRCORDER handles data changes, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.
